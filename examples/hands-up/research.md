# Team building poster: research notes

## Audience
Customer care advocates passing a corridor wall or sitting in the break room. They see it from about 3 metres, for about 3 seconds, between calls. The same design also goes in the team chat, read on a phone.

## What works
Observations from looking at strong event posters beside template ones:

- One gesture or object carries the subject before any word is read.
- The headline is two short lines of plain speech, far larger than everything else.
- The facts needed to turn up (when, time, where) sit together in one quiet place, away from the picture.
- Two or three flat colours, with a lot of empty space.

## Three ideas
See `concepts.png`.

| | Idea | Led by |
|---|---|---|
| A | The orange ring is the "O" of "off" in the headline | Words |
| B | A colleague high-fives a teammate coming in from the edge of the sheet; their palms meet on the ring | Picture |
| C | The ring is a table seen from above, with one empty seat | Layout |

## Chosen: B
It is the only one where a person is the idea and it still reads at thumbnail size.

- **A lost** because the person shrank to a token inside a letter, and the headline needed an awkward hyphen break.
- **C lost** because the people became dots, so it read as a diagram. "Your seat is saved" also implied a booking that nobody had confirmed.

## Decisions
1. **Orange is only the ring, and the hands meet on it.** Charcoal palms on orange are the strongest contrast on the sheet, so the eye lands on the high five first.
2. **The figures are solid charcoal shapes with round ends,** cropped at the hips by the sky band. One wears a headset pushed down round the neck, which carries "Headsets off."
3. **The facts are a write-in strip, not sample text.** When, Time and Where are real labels on ruled lines, with room to write one line under each.

Changes after the first render: the figures were narrowed and the arms attached at the shoulder; the headline was set on two lines; the supporting line moved clear of the ring; a headset cord was removed because it read as a stethoscope.

## Checks
- A3: `0 fail, 0 warn, 8 pass`
- 4:5 chat version: `0 fail, 0 warn, 8 pass`
- Looked at: the full render, the blurred view, the thumbnail, the greyscale and the grid overlay, for both sizes (`checks.png`).
- Copy on the sheet: "Team building", "Headsets off. Hands up.", "Time away from the queue, with the people who share it.", and the labels When, Time, Where. 20 words.

## Still needed
1. Date
2. Start and end time
3. Venue (floor and room, or an off-site address)
4. The activity, if it should be named
5. Who is organising, if they should be credited
6. Optional: a cut-out photograph of a team member who has agreed to appear (waist up, one arm raised). It replaces the drawn figure without changing the layout.
7. Optional: the official logo file
