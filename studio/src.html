Poster Studio · works on its own, or with any Copilot

Type it. See it. Print it.

Nothing to install and no internet needed. Type the words and the Studio draws the poster in the house look, checks it, and gives you a PNG and a PDF. Copilot can write the words for you.

01Make the poster

Type the words and press Make. The Studio draws the poster. Only what you type goes on it as fact: leave When, Time or Where empty and the poster gets a blank line to write on, not a made-up date.

02Or let Copilot write it

Paste the agent block into Copilot once and tell it what you need. It writes the words, picks the layout and gives you the research. Paste its whole reply here and the Studio draws the poster. A reply with three options gives you three to choose from.

03Research

Who the poster is for and why it looks the way it does. With Copilot, this is its research: the audience, what works, the ideas, the one it chose, and what it still needs from you. It is saved with the poster.
